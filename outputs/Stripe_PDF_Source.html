Executive Summary

Razorpay is a fintech company based in India, providing a suite of payment solutions for businesses, enabling them to accept payments online and offline. The company offers a range of products, including payment gateways, payment links, and payment pages, catering to various industries such as e-commerce, education, and financial services. Razorpay's mission is to simplify payments and empower businesses to grow.

Market Positioning

E-commerce businesses, Educational institutions, Financial services companies, SaaS and IT companies, Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs), and Large enterprises

Business Model

Razorpay operates on a transaction-based revenue model, charging a fee on each transaction processed through its payment gateway. The company also offers additional services such as automated payouts, subscription management, and cash flow management, generating revenue through these value-added services. Razorpay's business model is designed to provide a seamless payment experience for businesses, enabling them to focus on growth and development.
