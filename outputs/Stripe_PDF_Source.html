Executive Summary

Eastman Kodak is a highly diversified global manufacturer undergoing a significant strategic transformation from a consumer-centric photography brand into a specialized leader in advanced materials, chemicals, and industrial print technologies. Currently in a mature stage of renewal, the company operates across highly specialized niches including motion picture film, digital inkjet printing systems, and advanced chemical manufacturing. Headquartered in Rochester, NY, the company is focused on high-value industrial applications and professional imaging to drive sustained growth.

Market Positioning

Professional printing houses, commercial printers, motion picture production studios, industrial ink manufacturers, and OEM equipment builders requiring high-performance chemical and imaging solutions.

Business Model

Diversified B2B/B2B2C model. The company captures value through the sale of high-end industrial hardware (digital presses, platesetters), consumable high-margin materials (inks, emulsions, films), and licensing its iconic brand to third-party consumer product manufacturers (e.g., PIXPRO cameras).

Key Observations

  • Undergoing a major business transformation led by CEO Jim Continenza focused on industrial growth.
  • Strong presence in the motion picture industry, providing high-end color negative films like KODAK VERITA.
  • Significant pivot toward industrial inkjet technology and digital print infrastructure.
  • Maintains a powerful global brand identity, licensing technology and names to various segments.
  • Direct presence in the 'Advanced Materials & Chemicals' sector.

Products & Services

Digital Printing Systems
Solves workflow and cost inefficiencies for commercial printers by offering high-speed inkjet presses (PROSPER series) and hybrid imprinting solutions.
Offset Printing Solutions
Solves reliability and environmental concerns for large-scale printers through process-free plates (SONORA) and high-resolution thermal platesetters (MAGNUS).
Motion Picture Film
Solves the need for high-fidelity cinematic capture for production studios through specialized color negative films like KODAK VERITA.
Advanced Materials & Chemicals
Solves complex manufacturing requirements for OEMs by providing high-performance dispersions, inks, and chemical components.

Strategic Challenges & Pain Points

Market Commoditization
Traditional consumer film and photography markets have faced massive disruption, forcing a pivot to high-margin industrial segments.
Digital vs. Analog Transition
Managing the lifecycle of legacy analog products (film) while scaling complex digital inkjet infrastructures to meet modern printing demands.

Strategic Insights

  • Growth Opportunity: Expanding digital inkjet footprint into web-fed and hybrid imprinting to capture the commercial printing market shifting from offset to digital.
  • Growth Opportunity: Leveraging high-performance chemistry and advanced materials for industrial/specialty applications beyond photography.
  • Business Risk: Continued competition from technologically advanced Japanese and European firms in the digital imaging and professional print sectors.
  • Competitive Positioning: Moving away from commoditized consumer electronics toward high-barrier-to-entry industrial and professional chemical sectors.
